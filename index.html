Celadon

A quieter digital day.

Focus sessions, recurring routines and a moment to think before unlocking distracting apps.

Getting started

Open Celadon, grant Screen Time access and select apps or categories to block. Start a focus session and test it with a nonessential app.

Troubleshooting

Open Celadon to refresh blocking state. If blocking is not working, check Screen Time authorization in Settings. For unexpected restrictions, use iOS Settings to revoke access. Keep essential apps available.

Subscriptions

Restore purchases from the Pro screen or manage your Apple subscriptions.
