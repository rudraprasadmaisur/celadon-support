Privacy Policy

Celadon for iPhone · Last updated 25 November 2026

Celadon is a local-first focus tool. No account, advertising, third-party tracking SDK or developer-operated analytics service is included in the current app.

Screen Time permission

With your permission, Celadon uses Apple Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity to apply restrictions to the apps, categories and websites you select. These selections are represented by privacy-preserving tokens. Celadon does not read messages, keystrokes or app contents or send these selections to a developer server. You can revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings.

Data on your device

Celadon stores your block selections, schedules, optional focus intentions, session history, daily counts and subscription entitlement state in local app storage shared with its Screen Time extensions. Celadon does not upload this information to a developer-operated service. Your device and backup settings are controlled by Apple. You can remove the app and its local data; device backups may be retained according to your settings.

Purchases and notifications

Apple processes subscription purchases and payment details through the App Store. Celadon verifies StoreKit transactions to enable Pro. Apple handles personal information under its privacy policy. If you authorize notifications, Celadon schedules them locally on your device.

Support

If you choose to contact support, information you voluntarily send may be used to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive information or Screen Time selections.

Children

Celadon is intended for individual self-management, not parental monitoring of other people.

Contact and changes

Celadon is provided by Rudra Prasad Maisur. For privacy questions or requests, contact rudramaisur@gmail.com. Any changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date.
